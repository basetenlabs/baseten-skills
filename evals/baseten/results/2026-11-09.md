## Prompts and assertions

### 75: plan-loops-mode-and-billing

```text
We're budgeting a Loops LoRA fine-tune on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and need to know which availability mode it will run in and how it will be billed. Explain how Loops picks between serverless and dedicated, including the conditions that force dedicated, and what each mode bills for. Don't create a run or execute commands.
```

- Explains that the default mode is serverless only when the workspace has serverless access to the model and sequence
  length and the run's LoRA rank and sequence length are supported.
- Names reuse of another run's deployments as a case that stays in the dedicated mode, and explains that
  `scope=serverless` fails instead of falling back while `scope=deployment` reserves a trainer and sampler.
- Bills serverless per trained and sampled token and dedicated per GPU time, rather than promising token billing for a
  dedicated run.

### 76: diagnose-loops-org-capacity-limit

```text
Creating a Loops training run in serverless mode failed with a 429 whose details.limit was org_cap. Why didn't the SDK's retries clear it, and what can we do? Name the error the SDK eventually raises and our options. Don't create, deactivate, or modify anything.
```

- Identifies `details.limit=org_cap` as the organization's own limit on concurrent runs and samplers, not shared-pool
  capacity.
- Explains that the SDK retries a shared-capacity `429` with `loops_capacity` up to ten times before raising
  `LoopsCapacityError`, and that retries don't free capacity held by the organization's limit.
- Offers deactivating an unneeded run with approval or requesting a higher limit, instead of presenting retrying as the
  fix.
